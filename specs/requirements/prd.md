# Very Todo

## Problem Statement

People who just want to jot down things to do and tick them off are faced with tools that are heavier than the job. They need something that does only that, and keeps their list safe behind their own sign-in.

## Solution

A very simple todo app: a signed-in user adds todos and marks them done. Todos are stored in a database so they persist between visits.

## Actors

- User: a signed-in person who adds and completes their own todos.
